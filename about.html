About Clean Kampala Hub

Clean Kampala Hub is a proposed information website for households and small traders in Kampala. It helps residents find waste collection information, report missed collections or illegal dumping, and learn simple waste-sorting practices.

Our Coverage

The project focuses on Kampala's five divisions: Central, Kawempe, Makindye, Nakawa and Rubaga.

Project Team

We are Students from Victoria University Uganda.
