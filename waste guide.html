Simple Waste Sorting Guide

Separating waste at home can make collection, recycling and safe disposal easier.

Types of Waste

Biodegradable

Food leftovers, fruit peels and garden waste. These can break down naturally.

Plastic

Newspapers, cartons and clean paper. Keep them dry for easier recycling.

Sanitary

Used sanitary materials and similar waste. Wrap them safely and follow local disposal guidance.

How to Sort Waste

  1. Use separate containers or bags for different waste types.
  2. Keep food waste away from clean paper and recyclable materials.
  3. Rinse containers when practical and allow them to dry.
  4. Store waste safely until the agreed collection day.
  5. Never throw rubbish into drainage channels or open spaces.

Why Dumping Waste in Drains is Harmful

Waste can block water channels and contribute to flooding. It can also pollute water, attract pests and create unsafe conditions for people living nearby.
